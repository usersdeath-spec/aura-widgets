## The short version

Prism does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics,
no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. Everything you configure stays on your device.

## What stays on your device

Your placed widgets and their styles, your favourites, recents, and saved presets, your wallpaper
choice, and your purchase status — all stored locally in the app's private storage, and included in
Android's standard backup if you have it enabled.

## What leaves your device, and only if you ask

| Trigger | Sent | To |
|---|---|---|
| Placing a weather or sunrise widget | Approximate coordinates (city-level) | Our weather provider, [PROVIDER] |
| Placing a finance or crypto widget | The ticker symbols you chose | Our market data provider, [PROVIDER] |
| Purchasing or restoring | Handled entirely by Google Play | Google |

No request includes an account identifier, device identifier, or advertising ID. If you place none
of these widgets, Prism makes no network requests at all.

## Permissions

Each is requested only when you place a widget that needs it, and declining affects only that
widget.

- **Calendar** — read your events to draw agenda and month widgets. Read on device; never
  transmitted.
- **Approximate location** — choose which forecast to show. City-level only.
- **Activity recognition** — read the step count your phone already keeps.
- **Notification access** — read the current media session for the Now Playing widget. Contents are
  never stored or transmitted.

## Children

Prism is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete on our side.
Uninstalling removes everything Prism stored. Purchase records are held by Google under their
policy.

## Changes

Material changes will be noted in the changelog and here, with the date updated. We will not begin
collecting data without a new version and a clear notice.
