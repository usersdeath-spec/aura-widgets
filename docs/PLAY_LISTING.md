# Prism — Play Store Listing

Everything here is a draft for review, not final copy. Anything making a factual claim (battery,
counts, permissions) is asserted somewhere in the codebase, so the listing and the app cannot drift.

## Title options

Play allows 30 characters. Ranked:

1. **Prism — Widgets & Wallpapers** (29) — recommended. Leads with the brand, states the category.
2. Prism: Home Screen Studio (25) — stronger positioning, weaker keyword coverage.
3. Prism Widgets (14) — cleanest, and the one to use if the brand ever carries itself.

Deliberately avoided: keyword-stuffed titles ("Widgets Pro — 700+ Widgets, Wallpapers, Themes").
They rank slightly better and signal a low-quality app to exactly the audience willing to pay once.

## FAQ

**Is this a subscription?** No. One payment, everything unlocked, including future updates.

**Are there ads?** No, and there never will be.

**Is anything locked behind extra purchases?** No. There is one product and it unlocks the whole
app.

**Will my widgets keep working if I go offline?** Yes. Your purchase is cached on device, and
everything except weather, finance, and crypto works with no network at all.

**Does Prism drain my battery?** Clocks use the system tick that already fires. Battery widgets are
event-driven. Everything else shares one 15-minute task, and if nothing you've placed needs it,
Prism schedules nothing.

**Why does it ask for calendar/location permission?** Only when you place a widget that needs it,
and only for that widget. Declining leaves everything else working.

**Do you collect my data?** No accounts, no analytics, no trackers. Wallpaper colour matching runs
on your device.

**Can I use these widgets with my own wallpaper?** Yes. Setups suggest a wallpaper; nothing forces
one.

**Will you add more?** Yes — new families, wallpapers, and seasonal collections, all included.

**Can I get a refund?** Play's standard refund window applies, and we won't argue with anyone
inside it.

**Do I need a specific launcher?** No. Prism works with any launcher that supports standard Android
widgets.
