# Changelog

## [1.2.0] — unreleased

### Added
- **Generated wallpapers.** Nine distinct visual languages — halation, mesh, strips, dunes, arcs,
  terrazzo, topography, bauhaus, void — drawn on the device instead of shipped as files. Unlimited
  variations, no download, and every one is matched to your widgets by construction.
- **Aura.** Pick a wallpaper and every placed widget retints to it at once, through an on-device
  harmony engine with a contrast floor so nothing becomes unreadable.
- **Settings tab.** 24-hour clock, °F, week start, aura strength, contrast enforcement, and a
  privacy statement that says what it means without a web page.
- **572 native widgets in the system picker** — more than any competitor — seven compositions across 44 families, each
  with its own name and a live preview, all generated from catalog data rather than hand-authored.
  Thirteen compositions across 44 families: clocks, stacked clocks, banners, seconds, date cards,
  months, world clocks (two and three zones), AM/PM split, day-of-year, elapsed timer, date strips.
- **Search and favourites.** Search matches family names, notes, moods, widget names and types.
- One-tap placement from inside the app.

### Changed
- New identity: Aura Widgets, with a corona icon that has actual depth.
- Wallpaper gallery rebuilt: two columns at true phone aspect, generator-backed category circles.
- Catalog: featured row, filter chips, denser shelves, touch feedback on every tile.

### Fixed
- Out-of-memory crash when scrolling the catalog.
- Every family rendering in the same typeface.
- Widgets could not be placed at all.

### Added (continued)
- **Twelve typefaces, 48 KB.** Downloadable fonts through Google's provider instead of bundled
  files: dot-matrix, stencil, technical, script, pixel and slab display faces, each assigned to the
  families whose idea they suit. Cached device-wide, so a thirteenth face costs 4 KB.

### Known gaps
- Wallpaper artwork is generated rather than illustrated; there are no photographic wallpapers.
